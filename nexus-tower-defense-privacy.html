Privacy Policy

Nexus Tower Defense
Package: com.eduardhirsch.ehnexustd
Developer: Eduard Hirsch
Effective date: 1 June 2026

This policy describes how Nexus Tower Defense ("the app", "we") handles information when you use the Android version distributed on Google Play. The app is a single-player tower defense game. It shows ads provided by Google AdMob, and offers optional Google sign-in to back up your progress to the cloud.

Summary

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • No sign-in is required to play; Google sign-in is optional and only used for cloud save.
  • Gameplay progress is stored on your device, and also synced to your Google account if you sign in.
  • The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. To serve, cap, and measure these ads, AdMob may collect and use a device advertising identifier and ad-interaction data. This processing is carried out by Google as described in How Google uses advertising data and the AdMob policies.

Personalized vs non-personalized ads. In the EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and Canada, the app shows Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form on first launch. Personalized ads are served only if you consent; otherwise the app requests non-personalized ads. Outside those regions, ads may be personalized in line with Google's policies.

Your controls. You can reopen the consent choice in the app via Settings → Privacy → Manage consent. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device's Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Cloud save & Google Play Games

Signing in with Google Play Games is optional and enables cloud save. When you are signed in, the app uploads your game progress to a saved-game snapshot stored under your Google account — including scores, unlocks, in-game settings, and your ad-consent choice — so your progress survives a reinstall or new device. We do not receive this data on our own servers; it is held within Google Play Games Services.

This processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play Games Services. If you do not sign in, no progress leaves your device.

You can delete your cloud save from within the app (the in-game wipe-cloud option), or sign out to stop syncing.

Information stored on your device

The app saves game settings and progress (for example scores, unlocks, and preferences) in private app storage on your device. This data stays on your device unless you clear app data, uninstall the app, use a device backup feature provided by your phone manufacturer or Google, or sign in to Google Play Games — in which case it is also synced to your Google account as described in Cloud save & Google Play Games above.

We do not receive this save data on our own servers. We only receive information you choose to share voluntarily (for example in a support email).

Network access

The app declares the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (network state) permissions on Android. These are used to load ads from Google AdMob and, when you are signed in, to sync your cloud save through Google Play Games. Core gameplay runs offline.

Android or Google Play may also use network access on the device for store-related functions when you install or update the app through Google Play. That processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

What we do not collect

The app does not collect:

  • Name, email address, or phone number through the app
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, photos, or files outside the app's private storage

Advertising identifiers and ad-interaction data are handled by Google AdMob, and game progress is handled by Google Play Games when you sign in, as described in the sections above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you remove it. You can delete it by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

If you signed in for cloud save, your progress also lives in your Google account. You can delete it using the in-app wipe-cloud option, by signing out to stop syncing, or by removing the game's saved data in your Google Play Games account. Advertising data held by Google can be managed through your Google Ad settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the app changes (for example if we add new online features or change how ads or accounts work). We will post the new effective date at the top. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
